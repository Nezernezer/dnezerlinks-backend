Welcome to Dnezerlinks (“we”, “our”, or “us”), accessible via dlinks.name.ng and our Facebook Messenger chatbot. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our website, mobile services, and Facebook Messenger bot.

By using Dnezerlinks services (including our website and Messenger chatbot), you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, phone number, home address, and password when you register.
  • Transaction PIN: A 4-digit PIN used to authorize payments (stored securely).
  • Messenger Identifier (PSID): Your unique Facebook Page-Scoped ID when you interact with our chatbot.
  • Transaction Data: Details of airtime, data, electricity, cable TV, bulk SMS, and wallet funding transactions.
  • Wallet Balance & History: Your account balance and transaction records.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, browser information, and usage logs for security and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your Dnezerlinks account
  • Process airtime, data, electricity, cable TV, and bulk SMS transactions
  • Authenticate you securely via Firebase Authentication and our Messenger bot
  • Link your Facebook Messenger account to your Dnezerlinks profile
  • Display your wallet balance and transaction history
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Improve our services and customer support
  • Send important service notifications (e.g., transaction success/failure)

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data in the following limited cases:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party payment and SMS gateways (such as VTU providers and BulkSMSLive) necessary to complete your transactions.
  • Firebase (Google): We use Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database to securely store and manage user accounts.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by Nigerian law, court order, or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • Secure Firebase Authentication
  • One-time secure webview tokens for PIN entry (expire after 5 minutes)
  • Limited access to personal data by authorized personnel only

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Facebook Messenger Integration

When you interact with our official Facebook Page chatbot (“Dnezerlinks data & pins”), we receive your Page-Scoped ID (PSID) and messages you send. This allows us to:

  • Respond to your requests
  • Link your Messenger identity to your Dnezerlinks account
  • Send transaction confirmations and service updates

We only process messages necessary to deliver the services you request. We do not access your personal Facebook profile beyond what the Messenger Platform provides.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information and transaction history for as long as your account remains active or as required by Nigerian financial regulations. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us.
